## Direction contract
seed: none (pinned) — brief pins headline, object, four selves around it, one CTA, signature transition.

THESIS: The purchase is a price tag held by four strings; each string is one of your selves. Refuses the split hero (copy left, product right) and card rows.
OWN-WORLD: Inherits the app world: ivory ground, ink, Instrument Serif display, Geist for decisions, the four identity colours (budget slate, goals plum, owned umber, deal amber) used only as thread colour and marks. A white die-cut hang tag with brass eyelet is the only object.
STORY: You want it (giant type split around the tag) → four threads converge on its eyelet → "But does all of you?" → ask them.
FIRST VIEWPORT: "You want [tag] it." across the middle; four selves along the top, threads fanning down to the tag; subline and CTA centred below; "Powered by Drex" quiet in the mast.
SIGNATURE: Pointer sways the tag around its eyelet; the nearest self wakes (thread pulls taut, question appears); idle, the council murmurs in turn. CTA: headline halves part, all four wake, selves travel down their threads into the tag, stage dissolves into the app.
RISK: Thread geometry depends on live DOM rects; must stay attached at every width and under reduced motion.
